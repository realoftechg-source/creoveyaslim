Help & Tutorial

Getting Started

1. Create an account. 2. Choose a credit plan and submit payment with a receipt. 3. Once an admin approves it, your dashboard and Studio unlock automatically.

Going Live

In the Studio, preview your camera, choose or upload a look, then click Go Live. Your transformed feed streams in real time.

Running Low on Credits

You'll see a warning banner once you're close to your limit. When you run out, streaming locks automatically — your dashboard stays fully accessible, and you can buy another plan anytime from Dashboard → Buy More Credits.

Need More Help?

Use the Contact Us form on the homepage, or message us on Telegram.
