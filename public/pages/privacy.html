Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ECO SOLACE GLOBAL LTD ("Creoveya") collects and uses information when you use Creoveya AI Live Studio.

Information We Collect

Account details (username, email), payment receipts you upload for verification, camera/microphone streams processed in real time for AI transformation, and usage records (streaming time, credits consumed).

How We Use It

To operate your account, process payment approvals, enforce usage limits, provide support, and improve the Service.

Payment Receipts

Uploaded receipts are stored securely and are only accessible to you and platform administrators for verification purposes.

Third Parties

We use Decart's AI API to perform real-time video transformation. Video is processed for the duration of your live session and is not stored by Creoveya beyond what's required to operate the Service.

Your Rights

You may request account deletion at any time by contacting support.
