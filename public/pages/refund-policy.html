Refund Policy

Credit purchases on Creoveya are reviewed manually before being applied to your account.

Before Approval

If your payment has not yet been approved, contact support via the Contact Us form or Telegram to request a cancellation.

After Approval

Once a payment is approved and credits are applied to your account, purchases are generally non-refundable, except where required by law or at our discretion in cases of verified error or duplicate payment.

Rejected Payments

If an administrator rejects your submitted payment (for example, due to an invalid or unverifiable receipt), no credits are applied. Contact support to resolve any payment issues.
